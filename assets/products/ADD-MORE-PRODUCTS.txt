PINKA — HOW TO ADD MORE PRODUCT CARDS

The marketplace is now automatic and unlimited.

1) Put the new thumbnail image in:
   assets/products/thumbnails/

2) Put the large preview image in:
   assets/products/previews/

3) Open products-data.js and copy the LAST product object.
4) Paste it under the last object (before the closing ]).
5) Change id, title, price, image filenames and text.
6) Save. A new card appears automatically in the 3-column grid.

The first 3 products show immediately. Product #4 onward stay under the “ALL • មើលទាំងអស់” button.
There is no fixed card limit; you can keep adding products.

Suggested image size:
Thumbnail: 900 x 1080 px
Preview: 1200 x 1500 px
