CURRICULUM

What you'll actually learn.

A step-by-step creator curriculum that moves from setup to content, growth, monetization preparation and long-term systems.

Join the Support Program →
01

Foundations — how creator platforms work

Understand the business model behind major content platforms, common eligibility requirements and the basic account decisions that affect long-term results.

02

Account setup done carefully

Organize profile, niche, region and creator settings so your account starts with a clear identity and fewer avoidable mistakes.

03

Content that holds attention

Learn hooks, retention, watch time, format selection and how to study your own performance instead of guessing what audiences want.

04

AI tools and faceless content

Build repeatable workflows for scripts, visuals, voiceovers, AI video and editing so you can create consistently without a full filming setup.

05

Growth, consistency and content systems

Create posting schedules, batch content, test ideas and use analytics to decide what should be repeated, improved or stopped.

06

Monetization and payout preparation

Understand the steps usually required before payouts, how to keep records organized and where verification or service support may be needed.

07

Account health and recovery basics

Build safer account habits, understand common restrictions and learn a careful recovery workflow when normal earning or publishing is interrupted.

Learn it in the right order.

The Support Program is designed around this sequence so each module builds on the previous one.
